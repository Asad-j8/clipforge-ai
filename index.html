Android Phone & Low-Memory Device Guide

Practical tips for smooth client-side video clipping on mobile devices.

⚡ Use the Native Pipeline

On mobile phones, choose "Native Browser Pipeline" (the default). It uses minimal RAM and encodes without downloading heavy WebAssembly binaries.

📦 Video File Size Advice

For phones with 4GB - 6GB RAM, clips under 200MB (approx 5 to 15 minutes of gameplay) process smoothly. For longer recordings, trim raw footage first.

🔋 Keep Screen Active

Do not switch tabs or lock your phone during the export process. Mobile browsers pause background tabs to save battery, which pauses video rendering.

🛡️ Browser Recommendation

Works best on modern Google Chrome for Android, Samsung Internet, or Brave Browser with hardware video decoding enabled.

Frequently Asked Questions & Limitations

Clear answers on how a 100% free, client-side video SaaS works.

Is ClipForge AI really 100% free with no hidden charges? ↓

Yes, 100% free forever. Commercial tools charge \$20 to \$50 per month because they upload your gigabytes of videos to expensive cloud servers (AWS/GCP) and pay per minute for cloud GPU rendering. ClipForge eliminates server costs entirely by executing all rendering directly on your own device's CPU/GPU inside your browser.

Are my private gaming videos uploaded anywhere? ↓

Never. ClipForge has zero backend servers, zero databases, and zero tracking endpoints. When you select a video, it is accessed strictly through the browser's local File API and processed in your device's memory. Even if your internet disconnects after loading the page, ClipForge continues to work offline.

How does the highlight detection work? Is it "real AI"? ↓

We are completely transparent: ClipForge uses an honest heuristic audio-peak and motion detection algorithm. It samples the audio track using the browser's native Web Audio API, calculates RMS volume decibels to pinpoint scream reactions, gunfire, and explosive spikes, and combines this with video frame motion differences. We do not pretend to have a magical cloud neural network; instead, we give you transparent excitement scores and let you easily preview and adjust the timestamps before exporting.

Can I publish this on GitHub Pages for free? ↓

Yes! This entire application consists of static HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript, with a service worker (`coi-serviceworker.min.js`) included to ensure Cross-Origin Isolation headers on GitHub Pages. You can host it for free on your GitHub repository with zero server fees.

Why did my export fail or run slowly on a 2-hour 4K recording? ↓

Web browsers have a hard memory ceiling (typically 1GB to 2GB RAM per tab on mobile Android, up to 4GB on desktop). A raw 2-hour 4K video requires massive RAM to decode in browser memory. For best results on mobile, use clips under 15 minutes and 1080p or 720p resolution.

Can I add custom captions and gaming punchlines? ↓

Yes! We built a dedicated Offline Punchline Studio. You can type custom hooks (e.g., "NO WAY HE HIT THAT! 💀"), choose from gaming presets, pick custom styling (Neon Yellow, Cyber Cyan, Danger Red), and burn the text directly into your exported 9:16 Shorts.
